# Promofy at SBC Summit 2026

This repository contains the informational landing page for Promofy's campaign at the 2026 SBC Summit in Lisbon. The page serves as the central hub for attendees to learn about Promofy Spark, explore our platform, and schedule face-to-face meetings with our team during the event.

## About the Campaign

Promofy is heading to the SBC Summit to connect with operators and technology partners who are looking for new ways to improve acquisition, engagement, retention, and player experiences.

Through this campaign page, we are showcasing how operators use Promofy Spark to turn live player behavior, traffic, and engagement moments into meaningful, measurable journeys.
